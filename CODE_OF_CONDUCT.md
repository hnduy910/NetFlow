# Code of Conduct

NetFlow welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who participates in the project is expected to help keep discussions constructive, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and calmly.
- Welcome corrections and different perspectives.
- Protect private information and respect project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith interaction.
- Sharing credentials, private keys, or other sensitive material.

## Enforcement

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile or repository security channel. Please include links or other context when it is safe to do so. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to protect the project community.
